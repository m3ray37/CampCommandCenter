# 🏕️ Pop-Up Camper Packing & Trip Planner

An interactive web-based checklist system for organizing camping trips with your Coleman Americana pop-up camper. Track packing, meals, safety checks, and manage your departure day hitch-up protocol.

## Features

- **Master Checklist** (9 categories, 100+ customizable items)
  - Pre-trip maintenance & systems checks
  - Kitchen & dining essentials
  - Sleep & interior comfort
  - Campsite setup & utility gear
  - Emergency, tools & repair kit
  - Outdoor gear & recreation
  - Clothing & personal items
  - House prep & pet security
  - Final hitch-up & departure protocol

- **3-Day Meal Planner**
  - Plan breakfasts, lunches, and dinners for each day
  - Add/remove meal days for longer trips (up to 7 days)
  - Pre-trip grocery checklist with batch prep notes
  - Print grocery list only (without meals)
  - Trip-wide snacks & desserts section

- **Hitch & Departure Safety Wizard**
  - Step-by-step safety checks before towing
  - Interior/roof prep, exterior breakdown, hitch connection phases
  - Completion banner when all safety checks pass

- **Readiness Analytics Dashboard**
  - Overall completion rate (doughnut chart)
  - Category-by-category progress (bar chart)
  - Detailed breakdown matrix

- **Data Persistence**
  - All progress saves automatically to browser storage
  - Pick up where you left off next time
  - Reset option available
